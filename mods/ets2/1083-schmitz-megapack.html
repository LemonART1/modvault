About this mod

This comprehensive trailer pack includes four distinct trailer models and five types of cargo to haul across Europe. You get a highly customizable experience with support for personal tuning and the ability to paint your trailer any color you desire.

  • Includes four different trailer types for varied hauling needs.
  • Features high-quality baked ambient occlusion for realistic lighting effects.
  • Comes with 60 unique skins for both reefer and curtain trailers.
  • Fully compatible with trailer cables for enhanced immersion.
  • Mod is completely autonomous and supports user-defined skinning.
