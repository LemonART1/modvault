About this mod

This mod brings a detailed Mercedes-Benz Axor to your fleet with a high level of interior and exterior fidelity. It captures the essence of the classic heavy hauler while ensuring it fits seamlessly into the modern game environment.

  • Includes the authentic LX cabin configuration for drivers.
  • Features a standard 4x2 chassis setup for hauling.
  • Comes with custom engine sounds and a unique interior.
  • Fully integrated with SiSL's Mega Pack for customization.
  • Supports standard SCS license plates and trailer cables.
