About this mod

Expand your logistics fleet with this comprehensive collection of specialized trailers tailored for European trucking operations. You get a wide variety of cargo haulers including tipper, refrigerated, container, curtain, and food tank variants.

  • Features distinct, high-quality skins for every included trailer type.
  • Offers extensive customization options for personalized rig setups.
  • Includes specific container configurations compatible with SpecTransGroup.
  • Supports online World of Trucks loads for tanker, reefer, and curtain trailers.
