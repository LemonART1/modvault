About this mod

This mod increases the speed bonuses provided by tier one and two fins. It also improves the efficiency of the wakemaker item to help you travel faster. The changes are balanced to provide a noticeable boost without breaking the game loop. Installation is simple and involves placing the files into your mods folder.
