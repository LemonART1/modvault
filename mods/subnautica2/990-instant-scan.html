About this mod

Forget about sitting through those tedious scanning animations every time you analyze a new blueprint or creature. This tweak forces the progress bar to fill the moment you start scanning, letting you gather data and move on with your exploration without a second of downtime.

  • Enables near-instant data collection for all scan targets.
  • Removes the lengthy waiting period of the default process.
  • Compatible with drag-and-drop installation via UE4SS Mod Manager.
  • Multiplayer functionality remains untested and potentially unstable.
