About this mod

This overhaul turns the quiet UC Distribution Center in New Atlantis into a buzzing, authentic logistics hub. It replaces the empty interior with a functional layout populated by new personnel and gear, making the facility finally feel like it belongs to the United Colonies military.

  • Staff includes two Quartermasters, a Chief Quartermaster, and visiting UC crew members.
  • The interior features a visual refresh with UC orange and blue themes instead of plain grey.
  • Adds immersive details like wall-mounted weapon racks, packed shelving, and specialized storage crates.
  • Provides non-theft access to Vanguard Officer Uniforms and full Vanguard Spacesuit sets.
  • Script-free design ensures 100% compatibility with existing save files and minimal performance impact.
