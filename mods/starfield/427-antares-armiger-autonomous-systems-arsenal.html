About this mod

This mod introduces deployable combat drones that can be launched directly from your ship during space battles. You can purchase a specialized micro-factory weapons hab to manufacture these robotic units using your ship inventory. The system features two unique drone types and integrates with the scavenger perk for salvaging valuable components. Be aware that these systems are considered contraband and will be treated as illegal throughout the Settled Systems.
