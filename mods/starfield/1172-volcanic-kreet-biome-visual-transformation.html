About this mod

Refresh your playthroughs with this complete cosmetic overhaul of Kreet, turning the moon into a dramatic, lava-filled landscape without touching any gameplay mechanics. It replaces textures across all three major biomes and adjusts the atmosphere so the new lighting remains stunning regardless of the time of day.

  • Glowing lava is added to the Volcanic and Mountain biomes for a striking aesthetic update.
  • Mountains feature unique orange rocks and volcanic mineral deposits to stand out from other terrain.
  • The Frozen Volcanic Biome is transformed into a vast, bioluminescent blue ice field.
  • Custom low-fog settings and color grading ensure the terrain looks deep and atmospheric.
  • Weather patterns are modified to interact with the new glow effects, particularly during sunrise and sunset.
