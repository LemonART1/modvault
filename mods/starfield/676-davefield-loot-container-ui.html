About this mod

Cleans up the loot box and corpse UI with quick-glance icons and a credit symbol on valuable items, across both ship and ground loot. It's a small, safe-to-remove change to the vanilla interface - though pulling it mid-playthrough may cause a brief load shift - and it isn't compatible with other mods touching the hudrolloveractivation or shiphudquickcontainer files.
