About this mod

Track down dangerous criminals across settlements and major cities using a narrative system powered by AI-driven backstories. You gain leads by reading dataslates or visiting the Outlaw Terminal in The Broken Spear bar at Cydonia.

  • 100 procedurally assembled bounty quest chains featuring named gangs and crews.
  • Dynamic location integration utilizes your current library of installed POIs.
  • Earn unique legendary helmets, packs, and spacesuits as quest rewards.
  • Encounters and loot are fully level-scaled to your current progression.
  • Narrative content uses ChatGPT to generate lore-friendly motives and backstories.
