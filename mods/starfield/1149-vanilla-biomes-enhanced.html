About this mod

Experience Starfield's planetary landscapes with a major overhaul that makes exploration feel significantly more natural and less cluttered. This mod breathes new life into world generation by adjusting densities, flora sizes, and creature behaviors across various biomes.

Note that this mod may affect existing outpost placements, and it is strictly incompatible with Fantastical Frontiers or other mods that modify rocks and fauna.

  • Increases vegetation variety with randomized tree and shrub sizing.
  • Streamlines traversal by reducing rock clutter and footprint sizes.
  • Overhauls fauna systems to prevent mixed-pack spawns and improve group sizing.
  • Enhances predator and prey interactions with more realistic hunting and defense logic.
  • Adds NPC markers to fill out empty spaces for better landscape reactivity.
