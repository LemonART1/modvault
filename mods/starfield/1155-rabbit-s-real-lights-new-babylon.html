About this mod

This lighting overhaul breathes new life into New Babylon by implementing over 100 hand-placed light sources and objects throughout the city. The mod meticulously corrects existing lighting inconsistencies while completely redesigning the visual atmosphere of walkways and lanterns.

  • Adds over 100 hand-placed light sources and objects.
  • Improves lighting for multiple city buildings.
  • Overhauls the aesthetic of walkways and lanterns.
  • Compatible with all external LUTs and reshade presets.
  • Should be placed at the very end of your load order.
