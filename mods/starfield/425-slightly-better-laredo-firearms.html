About this mod

This mod enhances the Laredo Firearms interior in Akila City to feel like a proper, secure gun store. It adds numerous weapon racks, display cases, and clutter throughout the main shop and storage area. A new NPC named Dusty D has been added to manage the shop and keep things clean. You can secure the counter area with a locked door to keep NPCs out of restricted zones.
