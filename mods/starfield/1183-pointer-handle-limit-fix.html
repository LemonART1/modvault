About this mod

Heavy mod loads often exhaust Starfield's default 2 million pointer handle pool, leading to crashes or missing game content. This utility injects a fix during the early startup phase that expands your available capacity up to 4 or 8 million handles.

  • Doubles the usable handle pool from 2,097,151 to 4,194,303.
  • Includes optional logging to track references exceeding the vanilla limit.
  • Compatible with game versions 1.16.236 and 1.16.244.
  • Operates entirely at runtime without altering game files permanently.
  • Validates handle generation and slot assignment to ensure stability.
