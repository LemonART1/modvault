About this mod

This mod allows players to fly significantly closer to planets and stars without impacting cruise travel speed. It adjusts specific orbital distance multipliers to ensure a closer view while maintaining the default travel pace. This approach avoids the speed reduction issues found in other similar mods by carefully balancing the orbit radius settings. The configuration provides an immersive experience that keeps transit times consistent with vanilla gameplay.
