About this mod

Transform your vacation experience on Porrima II with this comprehensive texture overhaul that turns the resort area into a stunning, bioluminescent paradise. By replacing drab vanilla ground cover with vibrant red moss and pure white silica sand, the mod creates a genuinely scenic atmosphere without touching underlying gameplay or quest scripts.

  • Features glowing organisms and fluorescent minerals scattered across the landscape.
  • Adds reactive bioluminescent plankton to coastal waves for a magical effect.
  • Includes dynamic low-fog weather patterns appearing 50 percent of the time.
  • Implements atmospheric Aurora in the night sky for Shattered Space owners.
  • Maintains compatibility with building mods since it avoids editing worldspace cells.
