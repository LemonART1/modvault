About this mod

Experience New Atlantis without constant transitions by replacing static wall textures with functional, transparent windows across multiple stores and points of interest. This proof-of-concept mod aims to blend interiors seamlessly with the city exterior, though it may interfere with specific questlines if installed mid-playthrough.

  • Replaces opaque wall textures with real glass windows on city structures.
  • Removes loading screens for major shops to increase immersion.
  • Adjusts city lighting and reflections to complement the new exterior-facing designs.
  • Fully compatible with existing LUTs, ENBs, and ReShade presets.
  • Requires installation while in space for maximum stability.
