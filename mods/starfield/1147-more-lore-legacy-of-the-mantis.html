About this mod

This mod transforms the Mantis from a quick side quest into a sprawling, multi-layered mythos integrated directly into the Settled Systems. It shifts the quest start to level 25, requiring you to track down rumors across the galaxy to truly earn your mantle.

  • Adds 20+ new data slates found on Spacers, Pirates, and Ecliptic enemies.
  • Features 5+ fully voice-acted logs recorded by a professional team.
  • Updates terminal entries and security logs to provide a more cohesive backstory.
  • Implements scripted leveled list injection for high mod compatibility.
  • Note: Conflicts with mods editing quest MS04 or the Mantis Lair.
