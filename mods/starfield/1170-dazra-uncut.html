About this mod

Populating Dazra with the scenery Bethesda removed for performance, this mod restores various objects to their intended places. It effectively fixes floating items like antennas by restoring their original bases and removes vanilla assets that caused clipping issues with the returned objects.

  • Navmeshes are fully adjusted to prevent NPC pathing errors.
  • Includes objects spotted in trailers and PackIns files.
  • Features a tweaked gate entrance for better vehicle access.
  • Landing pads now exhibit realistic burn marks.
  • Compatible with Unofficial and Community patches without crashing.
