About this mod

This SFSE plugin automatically sets your Starfield process priority to High upon launch. It runs silently in the background to ensure your system allocates better resources to the game. No manual configuration is required once the plugin is installed correctly. It helps maintain smoother performance during demanding gameplay sessions.
