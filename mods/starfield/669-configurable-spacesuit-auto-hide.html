About this mod

A dedicated options menu lets you tune exactly when spacesuits auto-hide - in settlements, aboard ships, mid-combat, or out exploring a planet's surface. Helmet visibility and companion appearance sync to the same settings, so the whole crew follows your preference instead of just the player character.
