About this mod

This utility unlocks and allows you to purchase every item in the Extra Content Shop. It provides full access to weapons, costumes, accessories, models, and concept art. The mod acts as a direct substitute for the official in-game rewards unlock DLC. Please note that this script makes permanent changes to your system save file.
