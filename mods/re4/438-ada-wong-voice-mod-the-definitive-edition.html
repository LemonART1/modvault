About this mod

This mod replaces all of Ada Wong's English dialogue in both the main game and Separate Ways. It features fully integrated voice lines inspired by the classic Resident Evil 2 Reimagined performance. Players can easily manage the installation through the Fluffy Mod Manager. Optional subtitle support is also provided to match the new audio files.
