About this mod

This mod enhances Ada Wong's character model by increasing the size of her glutes for a more pronounced silhouette. The adjustments are applied to both her classic outfit and her specialized spy gear. It offers a natural look that fits seamlessly with the original game aesthetics. Installation is managed through the Fluffy Mod Manager for quick and easy integration.
