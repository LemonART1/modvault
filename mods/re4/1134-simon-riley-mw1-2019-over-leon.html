About this mod

Swap Leon's default and Pinstripe outfits for Simon Riley, featuring his iconic look from the 2019 Modern Warfare and Warzone releases. The mod includes his distinct Dreadwood and Last Breath attire styles. Keep in mind that this is a character replacement, so you might run into some visual quirks during your playthrough.

  • Replaces Leon Pinstripe and Default character models
  • Features Dreadwood and Last Breath outfit variations
  • Includes authentic assets from Infinity Ward and Activision
  • Expect minor clipping issues during regular gameplay
  • First-person camera clipping occurs when aiming with scopes
