About this mod

Keep your combat zones looking gritty by stopping enemy bodies from dissolving during Leon's campaign. This tweak ensures that defeated foes and environmental damage remain visible long after the fighting stops.

  • Bodies stay on the ground indefinitely rather than vanishing once the limit is reached.
  • Bullet holes and blood splatter textures persist on surfaces permanently.
  • The engine's decal limit is significantly increased to prevent premature cleanup of gore.
  • Aftermath stays until a scripted cutscene triggers or the chapter concludes.
  • Keep in mind that this is not compatible with the Separate Ways DLC and might slightly impact frame rates.
