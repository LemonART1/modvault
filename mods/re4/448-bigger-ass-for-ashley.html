About this mod

This mod enhances Ashley Graham by providing a fuller and more pronounced appearance for her Jacketless and Romantic outfits. You can easily enable included jiggle physics through the Fluffy Mod Manager for a more dynamic look. The mod is simple to install and fully compatible with other character modification files. Please note that minor clipping may occur in specific cutscenes due to modified skirt geometry.
