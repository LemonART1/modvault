About this mod

This mod replaces the models for Leon and Ada with Raven from Stellar Blade for the full campaign. It includes custom hair variants and physics for hair and suit components. The mod is designed for use with Fluffy Mod Manager for easy installation across both main story and Separate Ways modes. While some cinematic clipping may occur, it offers a complete character overhaul for your playthrough.
