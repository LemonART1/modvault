About this mod

This mod introduces a fully functional first-person perspective to the game, allowing players to toggle between camera modes seamlessly. It features custom aim stances and iron sights for every weapon to enhance the tactical experience. Players can fine-tune camera offsets and field of view to suit their preferred playstyle. The mod also includes advanced animations to ensure smooth gameplay during intense combat encounters.
