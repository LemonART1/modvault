About this mod

Swap out your standard medical recovery item for a refreshing can of White Monster energy drink. This aesthetic replacer modifies the textures, mdf, and mesh files of the First Aid Spray to create a more caffeinated survival experience.

  • Compatible with BIORAND and other mods that avoid altering the spray's original files.
  • Installation requires dropping the compressed file directly into the Fluffy Mod Manager directory.
  • Requires enabling the modification through the Fluffy Mod Manager interface before launching the game.
