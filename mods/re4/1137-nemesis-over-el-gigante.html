About this mod

Swap out the standard El Gigante encounter for the terrifying presence of Nemesis from the Resident Evil 3 Remake. This visual replacement modifies the boss model entirely to bring the iconic pursuer into the village. You will face Nemesis instead of the original giant, maintaining the classic scale and behavior while changing the aesthetic threat of the encounter.
