About this mod

Swap out Leon's pinstripe mafia costume for the iconic Ghostface look directly ported from Mortal Kombat 1. This pack features various mask and skin variations, including a custom gold mask, and offers multiple knife options to complete the aesthetic.

  • Replaces the Pinstripe outfit using Fluffy Manager for installation.
  • Includes numerous MK1 masks and skin color variants.
  • Features a Billy Loomis head option for an unmasked appearance.
  • Some materials may appear imperfect due to engine differences between games.
