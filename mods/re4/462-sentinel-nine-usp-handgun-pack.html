About this mod

This mod introduces the high-quality Sentinel Nine Heckler and Koch USP handgun to the game. It features a custom laser module, holographic red dot sight, and an extended magazine. You can choose from three distinct versions including a standard model and a suppressed MK23 variant. Each weapon model is fully animated and inspired by iconic firearms from popular military shooters.
