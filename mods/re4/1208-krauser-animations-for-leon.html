About this mod

This mod injects Jack Krauser's aggressive melee moveset directly into Leon's animations, giving your close-quarters combat a much more visceral feel. It is designed to work seamlessly alongside intense overhaul mods like Berserker Plus, Biorand, Chaos, and Fear Decent.

  • Replaces all standard melee animations with Krauser's signature moves.
  • Fully compatible with major difficulty and hardcore gameplay mods.
  • Requires Fluffy Mod Manager for installation and proper file management.
