About this mod

Swap out your standard RED9 pistol for the high-tech USM-AI used by the elite Hound Wolf Squad. This model replacement keeps the weapon feeling authentic to the squad's tactical aesthetic while maintaining compatibility with the original gun's animations and handling. It is a straightforward aesthetic upgrade perfect for players who prefer the Resident Evil Village special forces look over the classic Resident Evil 4 sidearm.
