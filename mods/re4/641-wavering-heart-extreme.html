About this mod

This mod provides an extreme gameplay overhaul for the Separate Ways campaign in Resident Evil 4. It increases enemy counts and modifies their placement to create a more intense combat experience. The mod also rebalances weapon statistics and adjusts dynamic difficulty specifically for the Expert mode. It is recommended to complete the standard version of the game before attempting this challenging edition.
