About this mod

Commemorating the Resident Evil 30th anniversary, this high-fidelity 3D model recreates Ada Wong's iconic look from official Capcom concept art. While the source material provided limited perspectives, this mod bridges the gaps with creative re-imaginings that maintain the authentic spirit of the design.

  • Faithful 3D recreation based on official anniversary concept art.

  • Stays true to the character aesthetic while adding flair.
