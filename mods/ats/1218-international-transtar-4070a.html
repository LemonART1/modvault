About this mod

Classic trucking enthusiasts will appreciate the faithful recreation of this legendary rig, which functions as a fully standalone vehicle available at the mod dealer. It offers a surprising amount of depth for custom builds, whether you are focusing on the driver's perspective or the exterior aesthetic.

  • Includes two distinct cabin styles and three chassis configurations.
  • Features a wide variety of engine options and paintjob support.
  • Deep tuning system covers both interior and exterior components.
  • Choose between four unique interior designs for your cab.
