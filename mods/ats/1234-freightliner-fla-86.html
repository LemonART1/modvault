About this mod

Customize your hauler with this classic Freightliner FLA 86″, featuring a variety of modular components to make it your own. You can pick it up directly from the in-game mod dealer to start your custom build.

  • Includes two distinct chassis options for different hauling needs.
  • Features one cabin type with support for various cabin accessories.
  • Offers numerous tuning parts including multiple exhausts, filters, and fuel tanks.
  • Comes equipped with custom engine and road sounds for better immersion.
  • Provides extensive options for exterior and interior cosmetic customization.
