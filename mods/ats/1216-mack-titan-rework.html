About this mod

Mahad110 and the team have put together a comprehensive update for the Mack Titan, focusing on authentic customization options for your heavy hauling needs. You get a solid, standalone build that feels right at home on the highway.

  • Features the classic Super Liner cabin style.
  • Includes a rugged 6x4 chassis configuration.
  • Engine options ranging from 425hp up to 600hp.
  • Eight distinct transmission types for better gear control.
  • Customizable with three different bullbars and interior flags.
  • Comes with a stock interior and a clean paintjob.
