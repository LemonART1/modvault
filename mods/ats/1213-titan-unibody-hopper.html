About this mod

This highly detailed trailer model by D3D features impressive 4K AO bakes that really bring out the structural depth. You get a functional dynamic loading and unloading experience that integrates smoothly with current ATS map expansions. It is fully ownable and includes two separate bonus packs to enhance your visual customization options.

  • Modeled entirely by D3D with high-resolution 4K textures.
  • Supports dynamic cargo loading and unloading sequences.
  • Includes a dedicated Glass Is Class lightpack.
  • Comes with a custom plate pack for personalization.
  • Fully compatible with the base ATS expansion maps.
