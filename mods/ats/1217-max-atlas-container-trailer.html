About this mod

This high-quality trailer mod by Habdorn brings the Max Atlas container hauler to your fleet with impressive attention to detail. It features a robust single quad-axle chassis and supports both 20ft and 40ft containers for a wide variety of hauling jobs.

  • Includes 49 uniquely textured containers and 4 distinct reefer units.
  • Offers 3 different wood deck styles to customize your setup.
  • Comes with a full set of 50 U.S. state license plates.
  • Features custom rims and tires, though you must use the included tires to prevent clipping.
  • Provides a massive selection of cargo options featuring real-world company names.
