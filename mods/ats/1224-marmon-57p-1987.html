About this mod

This classic 1987 Marmon 57P features a comprehensive model and texture overhaul in version 1.60. You get extensive customization options for your heavy hauler, along with improved GPS functionality and full cable support.

  • Four distinct cab configurations and a 6x4 chassis layout.
  • Five interior options featuring unique upholstery color variations.
  • Wide selection of engines paired with 10, 13, and 18-speed transmissions.
  • Integrated retarder support and extensive aftermarket accessory slots.
  • Dedicated tuning parts to personalize your truck's exterior look.
