About this mod

Elevate your rig's aesthetic with this premium set of aftermarket wheels, designed specifically for players who want their heavy haulers to pop in game. These assets offer a much higher poly count than vanilla options, ensuring your truck looks like a legitimate show vehicle rather than a stock fleet machine.

  • Features unique, high-poly aftermarket front and rear rim designs.
  • Includes specialized tires with custom rubber profiles and sidewall lettering.
  • Offers deep accessory support like hub covers and lug nuts.
  • Fully compatible with standard configurations and popular truck mods.
  • Engineered to maintain vanilla physics frameworks without causing stability issues.
