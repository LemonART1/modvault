About this mod

This mod brings the rugged feel of the 2005-2012 Mack series to your virtual fleet, featuring highly detailed models of the Pinnacle and Granite built from real-world references. You will find extensive customization options that let you replicate a personal rig with precision.

  • Includes over 10 bumper variations for both truck models.
  • Offers 28 unique chassis configurations for various hauling needs.
  • Features high-quality interior options for each individual truck model.
  • Adds a specific dumper variant along with a bonus pony heritage trailer.
