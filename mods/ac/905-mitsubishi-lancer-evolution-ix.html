About this mod

This meticulously crafted Evo IX brings the legendary JDM icon to life with impressive attention to detail and authentic performance dynamics. You can expect a highly responsive driving experience that balances that signature turbocharged punch with the reliable grip of a refined AWD system.

  • High-quality AO baking applied both inside and outside the vehicle.
  • Interior cockpit features a highly realistic, detailed dashboard setup.
  • Physics are tuned for a balance of accuracy and track-ready engagement.
  • Full support for Custom Shaders Patch features is included out-of-the-box.
  • Includes functional driver animations and a custom panoramic session start.
