About this mod

Experience the raw power of the 2025 BMW M3 G80, featuring a twin-turbo inline-six that pushes up to 523 horsepower and achieves a 2.8-second 0-60 mph time. This build by the Sayrx Performance Team brings incredible detail to your track sessions with realistic physics and three distinct tire sets to choose from.

  • Includes fully functional Android Auto and animated interior features like blinkers, wipers, and paddle shifters.
  • Features complete animations for all doors, the hood, and the trunk for added immersion.
  • Supports RainFX and includes LODs to ensure smooth performance on lower-end hardware.
  • Fully paintable within the CSP showroom and equipped with dynamic interior and gauge lighting.
