About this mod

This S13 V2 build is built specifically for those who demand precision on the drift track. It swaps the standard power plant for a turbocharged KA24DET setup that pushes 456 HP and 485 Nm of torque. The handling is dialed in thanks to a Part Shop Max coilover setup combined with a PBM angle kit.

  • Engine features a KA24DET turbocharged configuration
  • Suspension utilizes a Part Shop Max and PBM angle kit
  • Underglow effects are activated through Extra A
  • Strobe lighting is toggled using Extra B
  • Custom livery work provided by Livery Delivery
