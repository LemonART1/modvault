About this mod

This iconic ER34 brings a refined drifting experience to Assetto Corsa, complete with a powerful RB26DET engine setup. You get a balanced 465 horsepower paired with 483 Nm of torque, making it a reliable machine for technical tracks.

  • Engine output is centered around the potent RB26DET powerhouse.
  • Precision drifting is enabled by BC Racing BR suspension components.
  • Includes a PBM angle kit for improved steering lock control.
  • Exterior underglow is fully functional and toggled via Extra A.
  • Lighting strobes are available for customization using Extra B.
