About this mod

This conversion from VRDRIVING brings the classic Subaru Legacy B4 2.0 GT into Assetto Corsa with high attention to detail. The vehicle has been optimized to feel authentic on the track while maintaining a clean aesthetic.

  • Features full AO mapping on both interior and exterior surfaces.
  • Includes a highly detailed, functional dashboard for realistic driving.
  • Utilizes custom physics profiles specifically tuned by VRDRIVING.
  • Comes with dedicated CSP settings to ensure proper visual performance.
  • Supports full driver animations for an immersive experience.
