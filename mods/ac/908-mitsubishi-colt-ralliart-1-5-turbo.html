About this mod

Experience the compact turbocharged thrill of the Mitsubishi Colt Ralliart 1.5 Turbo on the track. This lightweight hatchback offers an engaging balance of precision and speed, making it perfectly suited for tight street runs and technical circuit laps. The interior and exterior are polished with high-quality AO baking for realistic lighting and depth.

  • Includes fully functional, realistic dashboard instrumentation
  • Features authentic physics tuned for responsive handling
  • Compatible with Custom Shaders Patch for advanced lighting
  • Comes with complete driver animations and seating positions
  • Includes a panoramic session start configuration by Daniel Nogal
