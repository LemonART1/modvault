About this mod

This dedicated race-spec version of the 2003 Honda S2000 brings a refined driving experience to your track rotations with a focus on authentic simulation. The model features high-quality ambient occlusion baking both inside and out for realistic lighting interaction.

  • Full realistic dashboard gauges and interior details.
  • Dedicated custom physics tuned for competitive circuit racing.
  • Includes specialized CSP settings for optimal visual performance.
  • Features custom driver animations for increased cockpit immersion.
  • Panoramic session start configuration provided by Daniel Nogal.
