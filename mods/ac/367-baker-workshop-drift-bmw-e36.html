About this mod

This BMW E36 is a street-style drift car with a turbo setup. It keeps the classic E36 look while adding a few useful visual options through the extras menu. The car features 442 bhp, 586 Nm of torque, and a weight of 1300 kg. Additional features include fog lights that can be enabled through the Extra A option. The car's performance and handling make it suitable for drifting.
