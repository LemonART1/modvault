About this mod

This custom mashup combines a VW Bora and Golf chassis, featuring a longitudinally mounted Audi inline-5 engine designed specifically for aggressive drifting. You get a highly technical setup that pushes boundaries beyond standard drift builds.

  • Custom front subframe with full Wisefab suspension components
  • Three adjustable engine maps ranging from 436whp to 610whp
  • Seven distinct tire variants including slicks and rain options
  • Fully functional CSP features like refracting lights and rain wipers
  • Interactive dashboard with working trip functions and odometer
  • Extensive custom extras including underglow, sunroof, and LED bars
