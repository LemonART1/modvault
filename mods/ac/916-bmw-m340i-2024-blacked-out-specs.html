About this mod

This G20-generation 3 Series build by Sayrx strikes an excellent balance between modern comfort and high-end performance. It integrates perfectly with CSP, offering a deeply immersive driving experience that handles just as accurately on a track as it does on a cruise.

  • Features realistic RainFX support for dynamic wet-weather racing.
  • Includes fully animated doors mapped to extra inputs A, B, D, and E.
  • Utilizes PBR materials and refractive headlights for authentic visual depth.
  • Optimised with specific LODs to ensure stable performance on low-end hardware.
  • Comes equipped with functional turn signals and realistic brake disk effects.
