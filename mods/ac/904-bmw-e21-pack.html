About this mod

Experience the authentic character of early compact sports sedans with this meticulously crafted collection of four classic BMW 3 Series variants. Ataberk Ozer Works has focused heavily on period-correct detailing and realistic driving dynamics to ensure these vintage machines feel right at home on the track.

  • Features the 315 in both US and Euro specifications.
  • Includes the performance-oriented 323i and the upgraded 323i Hartge RS.
  • High-end textures and shaders provide professional-grade visual fidelity.
  • Optimized performance keeps frame rates high during intense driving sessions.
  • Assets are original creations with protected KN5 files for security.
