About this mod

This mod removes movement restrictions for Arthur Morgan across the map. It automatically cures his tuberculosis and disables the New Austin sniper system. Players can now explore previously locked regions without dealing with constant wanted zones. The modification works as a standalone file by placing it directly into the game folder.
