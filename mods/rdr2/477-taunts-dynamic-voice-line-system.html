About this mod

This mod introduces a comprehensive system for Arthur and John to deliver context-aware taunts to enemies. It utilizes over 2000 hand-picked audio lines, including previously unused or cut content from the game files. Players can trigger taunts manually during combat or while observing defeated opponents to react to various states like dying or fleeing. The mod also features an aggression system that can provoke enemies into charging the player during intense gunfights.
