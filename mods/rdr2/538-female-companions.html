About this mod

This mod allows you to recruit various female characters from Red Dead Redemption 2 as permanent companions. Your chosen companion will accompany you on your travels, ride on your horse, and assist you during combat encounters. You can easily switch between different characters using the in-game selection menu. It is designed to work seamlessly with existing camp characters and offers deep interactions for your journey.
