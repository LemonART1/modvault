About this mod

Tired of being harassed for simply walking down the same street as an NPC? This fix stops those annoying false stalking accusations that plague crowded spots like the Valentine hotel.

  • Triples the time allowed before peds turn hostile.
  • Fixes overly sensitive lawmen in offices.
  • Prevents unwanted bounties during prisoner dialogue.
  • Allows for peaceful exploration without constant NPC paranoia.
