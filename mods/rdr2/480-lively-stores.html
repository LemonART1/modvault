About this mod

This mod adds ambient NPC behavior to various shops across the game world. Non-player characters will now browse items in general stores, gunsmiths, and doctor offices. Shopkeepers can also be toggled to visit other locations during their free time. You can easily adjust the visit frequency and settings using the included configuration file.
