About this mod

This mod replaces the standard tree textures in Saint Denis with vibrant cherry blossoms. The change creates a scenic and serene atmosphere throughout the city streets. It is fully compatible with existing weather mods for better lighting. Players can enjoy a fresh visual aesthetic while roaming the urban environment.
