About this mod

This mod breathes new life into the frontier by populating eight major settlements with children that spawn and refresh naturally as you explore. You can interact with these kids using the standard greet and antagonize inputs just like other NPCs in the game.

  • Adds children to Valentine, Saint Denis, Rhodes, Blackwater, Strawberry, Annesburg, Armadillo, and Tumbleweed.
  • Includes an .ini configuration file to toggle the mod on or off.
  • Allows you to adjust the spawn population density between Low, Medium, and High settings.
  • Requires ScriptHookRDR2 to function correctly within your Story Mode save.
