About this mod

Experience significantly more dynamic gunplay with these overhauled first-person animation coordinates that prevent your weapon from obscuring the reticle. By shifting the hand and gun positions for hipfiring, mounted aiming, and ADS states, the mod provides a modern, immersive feel during firefights.

  • Adjusts animation blending speed for smoother transitions between hipfire and ADS.
  • Includes a modified firstperson.meta file with optimized translation values for one and two-handed weapons.
  • Offers dual wielding support and custom animation movesets for various actions.
  • Requires a base FOV of 62 to prevent alignment issues with reticles.
  • Fixes specific weapon alignment bugs previously found on long-barreled firearms.
