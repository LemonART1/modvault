About this mod

This mod refines crime detection and law behavior to create a fairer experience without removing consequences. It focuses on adjusting specific meta files to ensure that NPCs realistically witness crimes before acting, rather than magically identifying the player.

  • Masks and disguises feel more effective during criminal activities.
  • Self-defense during brawls is less likely to trigger immediate crimes.
  • Train robberies no longer result in an instant wanted status.
  • NPC hearing and sight behaviors are tuned for better immersion.
  • Requires Lenny's Mod Loader for proper installation and file management.
