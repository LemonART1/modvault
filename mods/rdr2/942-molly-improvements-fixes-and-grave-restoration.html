About this mod

This mod breathes new life into Molly O'Shea by restoring cut content and drastically upgrading her character model's visual fidelity. It addresses the lackluster textures that left her looking out of place compared to the rest of the gang.

  • Quadruples material map resolution and upscales albedo/normal maps to 2048x2048.
  • Restores cut assets including her beta hair model, Gala Outfit for the Mayor's Party, and two Chapter 3 Camp Outfits.
  • Features custom HD textures for her necklace and fixes all vanilla neck seams.
  • Enables her fully functional, inspectable grave at the Beaver Hollow location.
  • Restores her beta eye color and adds higher resolution eyebrows and eyelashes.
