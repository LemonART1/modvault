About this mod

This conversion brings the sleek Mclaren 720S GT3 Evo into Los Santos with impressive detail sourced from Assetto Corsa Competizione. It includes full support for both Add-On and FiveM environments, making it a versatile choice for race-focused servers or personal gameplay.

  • Features a fully functional high-definition digital dashboard.
  • Custom signal lights allow for user-defined color changes.
  • Includes adjustable headlights and extensive tuning options.
  • Comes with crisp 4K livery assets.
  • Includes a template file for creating your own custom paintjobs.
