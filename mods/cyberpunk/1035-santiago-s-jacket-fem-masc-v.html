About this mod

This mod brings Santiago's jacket into your wardrobe with extensive customization for both male and female V. You get a massive variety of colors and optional accessories to mix and match.

  • Features 55 jacket colors, 30 hood colors, 25 strap variants, and 20 decal options.
  • Includes toggleable rolled sleeves that can be set for FPP gameplay only.
  • Designed with Garment Support and dedicated shadow meshes that adapt to your equipped toggles.
  • Offers automatic refit selection for major body mods like Angel, Eve, Gymfiend, and Solo 2.0.
  • Requires ArchiveXL, TweakXL, Equipment-EX, and The Community Palette Project to function.
