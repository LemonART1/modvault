About this mod

This mod restricts the Spillover perk activation to combat scenarios only.

  • Disables Spillover perk and Tier 5 Raven Microcyber effects outside of combat.
  • Prevents accidental enemy tracing while the player is currently in stealth.
  • Stops quickhacks from spreading unexpectedly when the player is not engaged.
  • Ensures controlled stealth gameplay by removing unwanted perk trigger conditions.
