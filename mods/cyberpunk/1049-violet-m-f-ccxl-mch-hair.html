About this mod

Give your V a fresh look with this physics-enabled hairstyle compatible with both masculine and feminine frames. It leverages the Archive XL and TweakXL systems to function in-game, and you can grab the MCH version if you want access to multi-colored options.

  • Supports both masculine and feminine body types
  • Includes physics for natural movement in the wind
  • Requires Archive XL 1.20.1 and TweakXL 1.10.7 to run
  • MCH Framework is required specifically for the multi-colored variant
  • Simple drag and drop installation into your game directory
