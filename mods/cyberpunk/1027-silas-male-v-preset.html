About this mod

Customize your male V with this specific facial preset designed for a unique look. The file is fully compatible with the Appearance Change Unlocker mod for easy implementation in-game. To install, simply drop the file into your bin/x64/plugins/cyber_engine_tweaks/mods/AppearanceChangeUnlocker/character-presets/male directory. If you encounter any issues or want to check for further updates, the creator encourages visiting their discord community for support and change logs.
