About this mod

Receive unique physical care packages from your Night City friends after completing specific questlines, delivered directly to your apartment stash points. The system uses a lore-friendly courier service called R.A. Delivery that functions off the Net for your safety.

  • Characters included in this first part are Mitch, Carol, Vik, Misty, and Nancy.
  • Misty sends non-linear messages regarding tarot card readings about your fate.
  • Each character provides unique rewards, and you may encounter hidden secrets.
  • Messages are fully gender-neutral, ensuring total immersion regardless of your V's pronouns.
  • The mod is fully compatible with Steam Deck and can be safely installed or removed mid-playthrough.
