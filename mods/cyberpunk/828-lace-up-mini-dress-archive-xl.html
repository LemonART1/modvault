About this mod

This mod adds a stylish lace up mini dress for Female V.

  • Requires Archive XL for the item to function correctly.
  • Compatible with Vanilla, PUSH UP, Enhanced Big Breasts, and Angel bodies.
  • Includes support for the More Realistic Butt body modification.
  • Simple installation by extracting files into your game folder.
