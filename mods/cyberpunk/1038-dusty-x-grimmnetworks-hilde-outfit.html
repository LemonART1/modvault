About this mod

This collaboration between Dusty and GrimmNetworks brings the stylish Hilde Outfit to Female V. You will need Archive XL, ACM, and Equipment-EX to ensure everything functions and displays correctly in your game. The mod includes extensive body mod support to fit various character builds seamlessly.

  • Supports Vanilla, Angel, EBB, EBBP, EBBPRB, and EBBRB body types.
  • Requires Archive XL and Appearance Change Menu to equip.
  • Optimized for use with the Equipment-EX framework.
  • Designed specifically for the Female V player model.
