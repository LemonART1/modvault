About this mod

This heavy-duty fire truck brings serious utility to your emergency service fleets with highly modular components and authentic performance specs. You get two distinct lore-friendly liveries for East and West Coast agencies, alongside a massive 8.5L Detroit Diesel engine.

  • Customize power outputs from 320 to 680 hp using four different turbocharger setups.
  • Choose between five professional-grade automatic transmissions including Allison, Voith, and ZF models.
  • Strip down the vehicle by removing the ladder, hoses, roof lights, or HVAC unit.
  • Tune the theoretical water tank weight via the configuration menu slider.
  • Create your own custom department designs using the provided UV map.
