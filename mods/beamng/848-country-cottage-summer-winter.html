About this mod

A country cottage map with matching summer and winter versions. Winter adds an accessible house with lights that turn on at night (toggle and adjust the snowfall from the menu) and a frozen river with a few nice touches, while summer just gets a fix for the orange textures.

  • Redid all the grass and fixed the streetlight lighting, adding it to the open house too.
  • Removed the lake's water.
  • Fixed a spot where dry asphalt met icy patches.
