About this mod

This project significantly enhances the original map by integrating new districts, roads, and functional facilities designed specifically for compatibility with the RLS Career Overhaul mod. You will find fresh opportunities for gameplay through added infrastructure and specialized delivery missions.

  • Features two new river bridges and a large logistics zone south of the airfield.
  • Includes a commercial district, a new gas station, and a functional sawmill.
  • Offers 17 Fast Response Event missions spanning 6 unique mission types.
  • Adds 11 player garages, a dedicated tuning shop, and 4 themed vehicle dealerships.
  • Integrates specific delivery tasks for gold, gemstones, and jewelry using the armored MD-Series.
