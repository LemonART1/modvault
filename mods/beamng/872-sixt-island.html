About this mod

This map offers a massive, diverse island experience featuring a South Beach Miami-style urban center complete with intricate nighttime lighting. You can test your driving skills across a wide range of terrain types, from dense jungles and arid deserts to steep mountain ranges.

  • Features a dedicated SIXT entertainment center with a race track, drift circle, and figure-eight layout.
  • Includes specialized driving areas like a challenging off-road course, mega-ramp jumps, and a parkour zone.
  • Provides versatile mountain driving with both winding asphalt roads and rugged dirt trails.
  • Built with high-quality PBR terrain and textures for a modern look.
  • Fully compatible with AI traffic and includes a festive winter variant.
