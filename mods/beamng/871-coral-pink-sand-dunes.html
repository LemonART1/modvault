About this mod

This map brings a 2-square-kilometer stretch of southern Utah's Coral Pink Sand Dunes to life with varied terrain. It offers a mix of steep rocky hills and vegetated areas for varied off-road challenges.

  • A closed-circuit race track modeled after Texplex courses sits centrally.
  • Explore an 11-kilometer route that traverses the entire dune area.
  • Navigate a maze-like network of mountain trails designed for length.
  • Every route and trail is numbered for easy navigation display tracking.
  • The 1.3.4 update resolves previous missing texture issues and adds skybox elements.
