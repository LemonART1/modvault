About this mod

This Mustang build focuses on authentic driving dynamics, incorporating real-world weight, engine characteristics, and aerodynamic profiles to mirror the actual vehicle. You will find a fully animated interior packed with high-quality materials and multiple seating configurations to choose from.

  • Original openable components and realistic baked headlights.
  • New navigation system paired with authentic engine audio.
  • PBR materials and lighting for superior visual fidelity.
  • Adjustable headlight projection and high-quality tire textures.
  • Precise physics modeling including varied engine and gearbox specs.
