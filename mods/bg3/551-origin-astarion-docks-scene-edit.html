About this mod

This mod modifies the endgame docks scene for Origin Astarion playthroughs. It allows Astarion to stay with Karlach instead of being forced to flee the sunlight. Players can now accompany Karlach to Avernus or support her during her final moments. The mod includes dialogue options for both Spawn and Ascendant Astarion paths.
