About this mod

This mod introduces the Marksman as a brand new homebrew class specialized in ranged firearm combat. Players can utilize unique mechanics like Endeavour charges and crafting to customize their playstyle with over fifty firearm combinations. The class features four distinct subclasses that allow for versatile tactical approaches on the battlefield. It includes high quality handcrafted visual effects, custom icons, and unique weapon models.
