About this mod

This mod introduces glowing, vibrant hairstyles to the character creator. - Adds both static and dynamic shifting glow effects to hair. - Glow colors are adjustable independently via the horn color menu. - Intensity can be modified by changing saturation and color brightness. - Fully compatible for use as a dependency for other mods.
