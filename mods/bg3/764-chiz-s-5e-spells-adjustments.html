About this mod

Rebalances 5e Spells to sit better alongside Baldur's Gate 3's own design: concentration requirements drop from most summons, spell durations line up with Larian's rest/turn logic, and targeting ranges get standardized to an 18-meter maximum. A number of bugs and stale data left over from earlier adjustment mods are also fixed. Requires the 5e Spells mod.
