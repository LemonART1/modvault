About this mod

This mod provides a complete retexture for various craftable items in the game. The assets feature a charming medieval aesthetic designed to fit a classic fantasy theme. It includes broad compatibility with popular map recolor mods to ensure visual consistency. Players can easily configure the settings to customize the appearance of specific items. The package is fully updated to support all items added in recent game versions.
