About this mod

This mod provides a complete visual overhaul for town buildings and interior spaces throughout Stardew Valley. It features seasonal decor changes that update the atmosphere for all four seasons including custom foliage and festive items. Players can customize their experience with configuration options for beam colors and specific building structures. The mod is fully compatible with popular expansion packs like Stardew Valley Expanded and Karmylla's Immersive Maps.
