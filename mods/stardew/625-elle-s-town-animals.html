About this mod

This mod provides extensive cosmetic replacements for nearly every creature and NPC animal in Stardew Valley. It includes hundreds of customization options for butterflies, town birds, local wildlife, and various unique event critters. Users can easily toggle or randomize appearances for these animals through a configurable settings file. The mod also adds support for numerous new creatures introduced in the 1.5 update.
