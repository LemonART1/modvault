About this mod

This mod provides 51 unique retexture options to customize your in-game cat. Players can configure different skins and optional collars for each of the three vanilla cat types. The package also includes updated menu icons that reflect your chosen appearance. Optional files are provided for users of the Adopt 'n Skin mod to expand your pet variety.
