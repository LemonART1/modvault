About this mod

This mod outfits 2B and A2 in the YoRHa Unofficial Ceremonial Attire ported directly from Stellar Blade. You can purchase the new look from the Resistance Camp Supply Trader once installed.

  • Requires the WAX mod by Woeful_Wolf to function correctly.
  • Must be installed manually; do not use NAMH or Vortex.
  • Overwrites original character files, meaning it is incompatible with other outfit mods.
  • Minor clipping issues are present due to the nature of the model port.
  • Text support is limited to English only.
