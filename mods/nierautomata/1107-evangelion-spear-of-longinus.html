About this mod

Swap your standard weapons for the iconic Spear of Longinus pulled straight from Neon Genesis Evangelion. This high-quality model replaces either the Spear of the Usurper or Virtuous Dignity with ease.

  • Features two distinct color variants including iconic Red and tactical Grey.
  • Includes the mass-production replica design for the Grey variant.
  • Utilizes an original model by cgasia for authentic proportions.
  • Fully compatible with the base game weapon replacement system.
